Horizon Contact

Horizon Contact: a Gamma contact centre alongside your phones

Horizon Contact gives teams that handle customer queries a cloud contact centre that works with Horizon. Users are enabled as agents in the Horizon portal, so you do not run a separate system.

  • Omnichannel contact in one platform
  • A fully integrated CRM
  • Integrations with Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics and Zendesk
  • Call queues and call-back options

What it does

Tools for agents and managers

Call queues

Hold callers in queues with sensible routing so none are lost.

Omnichannel

Handle more than voice, so customers can reach you the way they prefer.

Integrated CRM

Customer records and call history in front of the agent as they answer.

Popular integrations

Link Horizon Contact to Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics and Zendesk.

Call analytics

See missed calls and call-back activity. Akixi analytics adds much deeper reporting. See analytics.

Flexible teams

Agents can work from the office or home, and be added from the portal.

FAQ

Horizon Contact FAQs

Is Horizon Contact separate from Horizon?

It is a separate module that works with Horizon, sharing the same platform and portal.

Which CRMs does it work with?

Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics and Zendesk, plus an integrated CRM.

How big a team can it support?

It is aimed at small and mid-size teams. Larger operations may suit a different platform. See contact centres.

Can agents work from home?

Yes. Agents can be enabled from the Horizon portal and work from any location.
