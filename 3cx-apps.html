FAQ

3CX app FAQs

Do staff need to use their personal mobile number?

No. Calls and messages use the business number, so personal numbers stay private.

Does it work on Wi-Fi and mobile data?

Yes. The app makes calls over Wi-Fi or mobile data, and can be used from anywhere with a good connection.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and Android phones, Windows computers and modern web browsers.

Can staff use it in the car?

Yes. 3CX works with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto for hands-free use.
