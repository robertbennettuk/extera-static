Leased lines

Your own dedicated connection

A leased line is a private connection between your premises and the network, used by your business alone. It is not shared with other customers, so you get the speed you pay for, at the same speed uploading and downloading, with fix times backed by an SLA.

Extera, based in Banbury, Oxfordshire, orders, installs and supports business leased lines for sites across Oxfordshire and the UK.

  • Dedicated and uncontended bandwidth
  • Symmetrical speeds, the same up and down
  • SLA-backed fault repair
  • Easily scaled as you grow

Understanding the quote

Leased line cost: bearer and committed data rate

Leased lines are quoted with two figures. The bearer is the maximum capacity of the circuit. The committed data rate (CDR) is the bandwidth you choose and pay for. For example, a 100Mbps service on a 1Gbps bearer can be upgraded later without new infrastructure.

That is why a leased line is easy to scale: increasing your bandwidth is often a simple change rather than a new install.

Leased line or broadband?

Choose a leased line if your team relies on cloud apps, hosted voice or video all day, or if you need guaranteed fix times. Broadband is fine for lighter use and tighter budgets. See fibre broadband.

FAQ

Leased line FAQs

How fast are leased lines?

Common speeds range from 100Mbps up to 10Gbps, depending on location and need.

How long does installation take?

Often a number of weeks, and longer where new fibre has to be laid or landlord or council permission is needed. We give you a realistic timescale in the quote.

Can I change my speed later?

Usually you can increase bandwidth during the contract. Reductions are normally limited, so check the terms.

What equipment do I need?

We supply a configured router with the service, and can add firewalls and switches as needed.

What if I move premises?

Moving a leased line depends on the new address and the remaining contract term. Talk to us early.
