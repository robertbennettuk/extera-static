Where we work

Regions we cover

Our engineers cover mainland UK. These are the regions we work in:

  • London and the South East
  • South West England
  • East of England
  • East Midlands and West Midlands
  • North West, North East, Yorkshire and the Humber
  • Wales
  • Scotland

Closest to our base are Oxfordshire and the neighbouring counties, where visits are simplest to arrange. If you are in Northern Ireland or the Channel Islands, get in touch and we will explain what we can do remotely and what is possible on site.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you support us if we are not near Banbury?

Yes. Most phone system changes and faults can be fixed remotely, and an engineer visits when something needs hands on site.

Do you charge for travel?

Any travel or visit costs are shown in the quote, so there are no surprises. Ask us when you enquire.

Do you only work with businesses in Oxfordshire?

No. We are based in Banbury and work with businesses across mainland UK.

Can you handle a business with several sites?

Yes. We plan phones, connectivity and cabling across sites, with one point of contact.

How do I get a quote?

Call us on 01295 220 600 or send an enquiry. Tell us where you are, how many people you have and what you need.
