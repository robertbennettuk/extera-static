ISDN Switch‑Off Guide

Closing 31 January 2027: what stops working, your options and a simple plan to move without losing calls.

Book a free line auditCall 01295 220 600
The deadline

The UK phone network is due to close on 31 January 2027

The old copper telephone network, the PSTN, is being retired and replaced by digital voice over the internet. ISDN lines run on that network, so ISDN goes with it. The closing date reported at the time of writing is 31 January 2027, and it has not moved.

Openreach has said that around 350,000 business premises were still on legacy lines in September 2026 (ISPreview, September 2026). If your business is one of them, the time to act is now, because suppliers and engineers get busier as the date approaches.

What is affected

What stops working when ISDN and the PSTN close

ISDN2 and ISDN30 lines

The digital lines many office phone systems use to make and receive calls.

Analogue phone lines

Single lines used for phones, fax and other equipment.

Alarms and CCTV

Intruder and fire alarms that dial out over a phone line to a monitoring centre.

Lifts and door entry

Lift emergency phones and some door entry systems use a line to call out.

Card payment terminals

Older machines that connect through an analogue line.

Franking machines and fax

Equipment that dials a line to send data or documents.

Some broadband also depends on the PSTN. If your broadband and phone share an old copper line, check with your provider how it will be moved.

The plan

How to move without losing calls

  1. Audit

    List every line and everything that uses it: phones, alarms, lifts, card machines and fax.

  2. Choose

    Pick SIP trunks, a hosted system or a new system, based on your audit and budget.

  3. Order

    Place orders early. Lead times vary and demand rises as the deadline gets closer.

  4. Port and test

    Move your numbers and test calls, including out-of-hours routing and failover.

  5. Switch over

    Cut over at a quiet time with an engineer on hand, then cancel the old lines.

Checklist

A quick line audit you can do today

  • Count your ISDN2, ISDN30 and analogue lines
  • Find every phone number and where it rings
  • List alarms, lifts, door entry and card machines that use a line
  • Check your phone system supports SIP, or what an upgrade would cost
  • Test your internet speed and ask what happens if it fails
  • Find your contract end dates and notice periods

Dates and figures here were checked in October 2026 and can change. Confirm the current position with your provider.

FAQ

Common questions

Will my ISDN line stop on 31 January 2027?

ISDN runs on the PSTN, which is due to close on that date. Some providers are moving customers earlier, so ask yours when your lines will be withdrawn.

Can I keep my phone numbers?

Yes. Numbers can be moved, or ported, to SIP trunks or a hosted phone system. We plan the move so calls keep working.

Do I need new phones?

It depends. Analogue phones can often keep working with an adapter, and many digital handsets work with a hosted system. We check each handset in the audit.

What about my alarm and card machines?

Ask the equipment supplier about a digital version. Many can use broadband or a 4G connection. We can help find the right connection.

What is Openreach's emergency voice service?

Openreach has said it will run a temporary, limited service after January 2027 for lines that have not moved. It is a last resort, not a plan, and it costs more than a modern digital service.
