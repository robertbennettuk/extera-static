Leased Line Cost

What a business leased line costs, why quotes differ and how to keep the price sensible.

Get a leased line quoteCall 01295 220 600
The short answer

Leased line prices depend on speed, location and contract length

A leased line is dedicated to your business, so the price reflects the work to build and run it. Two quotes for the same speed can differ a lot, and the reasons are usually in the small print.

Speed

A higher committed data rate costs more. Many businesses pay for more speed than they use.

Location

The distance to the nearest network and any new fibre to be built to your site.

Contract length

Longer terms generally lower the monthly price but lock you in.

Service level

Faster fix times and 24x7 cover cost more than standard cover.

Resilience

A second line or backup link adds cost but protects against a fault.

Extras

A router, static IP addresses and voice priority may be included or charged for.

Saving money

Ways to keep the cost sensible

  • Choose the committed speed you need, then check you can upgrade later
  • Compare several carriers rather than one, because coverage and build costs differ
  • Check whether full fibre broadband would meet your needs for less
  • Ask for the price on several contract lengths
  • Put voice and data on the same line with SIP trunks
  • Plan a backup connection so a fault does not stop the business

FAQ

Common questions

How much does a leased line cost?

It depends on speed, location and contract length. We compare carriers for your address and send a written quote showing installation and monthly costs.

Why is installation sometimes expensive?

If fibre has to be built to reach your building, the carrier may charge for the construction. We ask for the cost up front.

How long does a leased line take?

Longer than broadband, because building and testing take time. We give you a realistic date when we quote.

Is a leased line better than fibre broadband?

A leased line gives guaranteed, dedicated speed and a fast fix time. Full fibre broadband is cheaper and suits many offices. We help you decide.

Can I get a leased line in Banbury or Oxfordshire?

Availability depends on the address. Send us your postcode and we will check with the carriers.
