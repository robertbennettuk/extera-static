FAQ

3CX video and chat FAQs

Is video conferencing really included?

Yes. It is part of 3CX, with no per-user fees or time caps, for up to 250 participants.

Do guests need to install anything?

No. Guests join from a link in their browser, or dial in by phone.

Can we run webinars?

Yes. You can run large meetings and webinars, record them, and stream to YouTube.

Can several agents share WhatsApp or SMS?

Yes. Messages can be routed to a queue of agents so the workload is shared and none are missed.

Does live chat work with our website?

Yes. It works with WordPress, Drupal and Joomla sites, and with custom websites.
